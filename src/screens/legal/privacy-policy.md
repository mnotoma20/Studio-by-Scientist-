# Privacy Policy

**Studio by Scientist**
Last updated: May 2026

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Account information:** Email address, church name, location, and church size provided at signup.
- **Usage data:** Service session activity within the App.
- **Audio data:** Microphone audio is processed in real-time for transcription. Audio is sent to OpenAI's Whisper API and is not stored by us.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To operate and maintain your account
- To provide Bible detection and transcription features
- To improve the App

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:
- **Supabase** — account storage and authentication
- **OpenAI** — audio transcription (Whisper) and Bible reference detection (GPT-4o-mini)
- **Bible API (bible-api.com)** — verse text lookup

Each service has its own privacy policy. We recommend reviewing them.

## 4. Data Storage

Account data is stored securely on Supabase's servers. We do not sell your data to third parties.

## 5. Audio Processing

Audio captured during live sessions is processed server-side by OpenAI for transcription only. We do not retain audio recordings.

## 6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at support@studiobyscientist.com.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify users of significant changes via the App.
