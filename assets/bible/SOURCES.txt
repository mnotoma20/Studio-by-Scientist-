Bundled public-domain Bible texts (work with no internet). KJV is ../kjv.json.

asv    American Standard Version (1901)             public domain
bsb    Berean Standard Bible                        dedicated to the public domain 30 April 2023 (berean.bible/terms.htm)
darby  Darby Bible Translation (1890)               public domain
ylt    Young's Literal Translation (1898)           public domain

Text taken from github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases (formats/json), converted to
[[bookName, [[verse text, ...] per chapter]], ...] and gzipped. Book names normalised to the KJV
names; spacing glitches in the source (e.g. "ForGod" in Darby) repaired. Every file has the same
31,102 verses / chapter structure as the KJV.
